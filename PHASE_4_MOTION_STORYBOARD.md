| 01 | load/scroll | headline reveal + small node arrangement | a system is waiting to be explored |

Motion rules: Reveal, Transform, Recomposition, Continuity, Hierarchy.
No scroll-jacking, parallax wall, particles, decorative blobs, or animation-only state changes.
